IGNOAPS
Old Age Pension
▾
Who Qualifies
BPL citizens aged 60+ years
Central Benefit
Rs.200/month (60-79 yrs), Rs.500/month (80+ yrs)
Key Conditions
BPL; NOT receiving any other pension
Documents
Aadhaar, BPL certificate, age proof, bank passbook
IGNWPS
Widow Pension
▾
Who Qualifies
BPL widows aged 40-79 years
Central Benefit
Rs.300/month
Key Conditions
Female, widowed, age 40-79, BPL household
Documents
Aadhaar, BPL cert, spouse death cert, age proof, bank passbook
IGNDPS
Disability Pension
▾
Who Qualifies
BPL persons with severe disabilities (>=80%), aged 18-79
Central Benefit
Rs.300/month
Key Conditions
Disability certificate >= 80%; age 18-79; BPL
Documents
Aadhaar, BPL cert, disability certificate (>=80%), bank passbook
NFBS
Family Benefit Scheme
▾
Who Qualifies
BPL households that lost their primary breadwinner
Central Benefit
Rs.20,000 one-time lump sum
Key Conditions
Breadwinner died aged 18-59; BPL; apply within 90 days
Documents
Aadhaar, BPL cert, death certificate, bank passbook
Note: States may add top-up over central amounts. Apply at: nsap.nic.in
